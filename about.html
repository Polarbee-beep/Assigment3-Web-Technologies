About the Builder

A passionate solo Minecraft architect with over 5 years of map design experience.

My Story

I started building in Minecraft back in 2021 just for fun, exploring voxel art, custom terrain creation, and structural design. Over the years, my hobby evolved into a professional freelance venture. Today, I work independently with multiplayer server networks and individual creators to construct high-quality lobbies, minigame arenas, and immersive custom worlds.
